# Figure 4 (drought_coincidence)

**Figure 4.** Scatter plot of modelled daily cooling makeup for the calibrated Falls Township hybrid against the same-day flow of the Delaware River at Trenton, expressed as the day-of-year percentile of that day's flow among the same calendar day in 2005 to 2024 [reuse_ready_model; usgs_nwis_01463500]. Only the 966 days with makeup above zero are drawn on the logarithmic axis [reuse_ready_model]. Percentile ranks use the mid-rank for ties, and February 29 is pooled with February 28 and March 1 [usgs_nwis_01463500]. Accent points fall inside DRBC drought warning or emergency periods [drbc_drought_page]. The basinwide table lists no warning or emergency stage between 2005 and 2024; its only row in that window is the drought watch of 23 November 2016 to 18 January 2017 [drbc_drought_page]. The highlighted periods are therefore taken from the text of the same page: the Lower Basin drought warning of 24 September to 31 October 2010; the water supply emergency of 2016, dated from Resolution 2016-07 of 23 November 2016 and closed at the end of the drought watch on 18 January 2017 because the page gives no separate end date; and the water supply emergency of December 2024 to June 2025, opened on 5 December 2024 (the date of the DRBC news release) and clipped at 31 December 2024 [drbc_drought_page]. Only the 2010 period falls in the season with makeup above zero, and it contains 5 such days [reuse_ready_model]. With 20 years in the window, each calendar day has 20 flows, so percentile ranks fall on 20 discrete values and the points form columns [usgs_nwis_01463500]. The dashed vertical line marks the 25th flow percentile; the dashed horizontal line marks the top 5 percent of valid days by makeup (1,123,853 gal/d or more, 361 days) [reuse_ready_model], and the solid horizontal line marks the 100,000 gal/d DRBC review threshold, which applies to the daily average over any 30 consecutive days [drbc_admin_manual]. The full-record 7Q10 of 1,795 cfs [usgs_nwis_01463500] lies below every daily flow from 2005 to 2024 (minimum 2,370 cfs), so its percentile equivalent is 0 on every calendar day and an arrow marks it at the left axis [usgs_nwis_01463500]. Of the top 5 percent demand days, 23.8 percent (86 days) fall below the 25th flow percentile, 0 percent fall below the 7Q10, and 1 day falls inside a drought warning or emergency period [reuse_ready_model; usgs_nwis_01463500; drbc_drought_page].
