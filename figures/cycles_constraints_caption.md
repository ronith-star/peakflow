# Figure X (cycles_constraints)

**Figure X.** Allowable cycles of concentration for a cooling tower supplied with secondary effluent from each of the 19 matched municipal plants [cycles_by_plant.csv]. Each row is one plant, sorted by allowable cycles; the open markers give the cycles at which the concentrated water reaches the silica limit of 150 mg/L as SiO2 [midkiff_1977, PDF p. 3; dogra_2023, PDF p. 5], the chloride level of 1060 mg/L carried by mixed carbon steel, stainless steel and copper alloy metallurgy [geiger_1993, PDF pp. 2, 4, 7], 15 mg/L orthophosphate as PO4 [geiger_1993, PDF p. 5], and a Langelier saturation index of 2.5 [geiger_1993, PDF p. 3] at 35 C bulk water with pH capped at 8.5 [vidic_2009, PDF p. 167; hem_1985, pp. 15, 19, 253]. The filled accent marker is the binding constraint, which is phosphate at 14 plants, the saturation index at 4 and chloride at 1 [cycles_by_plant.csv]. The dashed line marks the 4 cycles assumed by the cooling model [reuse_ready_model], and the shaded band marks makeup that already exceeds a limit. The right-hand columns give the allowable cycles C and the implied minimum return flow, defined as blowdown divided by makeup, 100/C percent [cycles_by_plant.csv]. Every row is marked assumed: the Water Quality Portal was not reachable [wqp_status.json], so silica, calcium and alkalinity are literature secondary-effluent values [vidic_2009, PDF p. 29; hem_1985, p. 73], chloride is DMR total dissolved solids times a chloride ratio of 0.34 [epa_echo; cycles_summary.json], and phosphate and dissolved solids are each plant's DMR median where reported [epa_echo].

**Citation keys.** epa_echo, vidic_2009, hem_1985, geiger_1993, midkiff_1977, dogra_2023, reuse_ready_model. Plotted values are in figures/cycles_constraints_data.csv.
