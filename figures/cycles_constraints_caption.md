# Figure X (cycles_constraints)

**Figure X.**

The chart shows the allowable cycles of concentration for a cooling tower supplied with secondary effluent from each of the 19 matched municipal plants [cycles_by_plant.csv]. Each row is one plant, sorted by allowable cycles. The open markers give the cycles at which the concentrated water reaches each of four limits. The first is the silica limit of 150 mg/L as SiO2 [midkiff_1977, PDF p. 3] [dogra_2023, PDF p. 5]. The second is the chloride level of 1060 mg/L carried by mixed carbon steel, stainless steel and copper alloy metallurgy [geiger_1993, PDF pp. 2, 4, 7]. The third is 15 mg/L orthophosphate as PO4 [geiger_1993, PDF p. 5]. The fourth is a Langelier saturation index of 2.5 [geiger_1993, PDF p. 3], evaluated at 35 C bulk water with pH capped at 8.5 [vidic_2009, PDF p. 167] [hem_1985, pp. 15, 19, 253]. The filled accent marker is the binding constraint, which is phosphate at 14 plants, the saturation index at 4 and chloride at 1 [cycles_by_plant.csv]. The dashed line marks the 4 cycles assumed by the cooling model [reuse_ready_model]. The shaded band marks makeup that already exceeds a limit. The right-hand columns give the allowable cycles C and the implied minimum return flow, 100/C percent [cycles_by_plant.csv]. Return flow is defined as blowdown divided by makeup.

Every row is marked assumed because the Water Quality Portal was not reachable [wqp_status.json]. Silica, calcium and alkalinity are therefore literature secondary-effluent values [vidic_2009, PDF p. 29] [hem_1985, p. 73]. Chloride is DMR total dissolved solids times a chloride ratio of 0.34 [epa_echo, cycles_summary.json]. Phosphate and dissolved solids are each plant's DMR median where reported [epa_echo].

The citation keys are epa_echo, vidic_2009, hem_1985, geiger_1993, midkiff_1977, dogra_2023 and reuse_ready_model. Plotted values are in figures/cycles_constraints_data.csv.
