**Figure X.** Annual cooling water and non-IT energy per megawatt of IT load for five cooling architectures at Trenton, New Jersey. Points are central values and bars span the low and high values on each axis. Filled circles are modelled on Trenton hourly wet-bulb temperature for 2005 to 2024 [noaa_isd; stull_2011; reuse_ready_model]; open circles take water from the LBNL simulated or reported site water usage effectiveness [shehabi_2024]; the open diamond is the Falls Township (AWS Keystone) filing, 135,000 gallons per day [falls_levittown_2026] times 365 divided by the calibrated 331.19 MW of IT load [reuse_ready_model], or 149,000 gallons per MW-year, at the assumed power usage effectiveness (PUE) of 1.2 [reuse_ready_model]. It coincides with the hybrid central value because the hybrid is calibrated to that filing. Energy is (PUE minus 1) times 8,760,000 kWh per MW-year, with PUE read from LBNL 2024 Figure 4.4 on page 46 (median as the central value, whisker ends as the range) [shehabi_2024]; PUE minus 1 includes all non-IT load, so it bounds cooling energy from above. The PUE rows used are the Large-scale waterside economizer (water-cooled chiller), 1.030, 1.118 and 1.226, for the evaporative tower; the Large-scale dry cooler with adiabatic assist, 1.055, 1.199 and 1.347, for the hybrid; the Midsize air-cooled chiller, 1.392, 1.828 and 2.324, because LBNL draws no Large-scale air-cooled chiller; and the two Large-scale airside economizer and adiabatic cooling rows, 1.010, 1.143 and 1.277 (water-cooled chiller) and 1.015, 1.168 and 1.332 (air-cooled chiller) [shehabi_2024]. Values were digitized from the figure raster at a resolution of 0.005 in PUE and 0.014 L/kWh [reuse_ready_model]. Modelled water scales with PUE; its range combines the lowest calendar-year total at the low PUE with the highest at the high PUE [reuse_ready_model]. Water for the water-cooled airside row is the Figure 4.4 site WUE, 0.00, 0.42 and 1.56 L/kWh [shehabi_2024], converted at 8,760,000 kWh per MW-year and 3.785411784 L per gallon [reuse_ready_model]; for the air-cooled airside row, whose simulated WUE LBNL describes as likely low, it is the 0.1 to 0.3 L/kWh that hyperscale operators report for similar systems, centred on 0.2 L/kWh (page 47) [shehabi_2024]. The air-cooled chiller uses no evaporative makeup and is plotted in the separate zero panel; the lower end of the water-cooled airside range is also zero and continues into that panel [reuse_ready_model; shehabi_2024]. Data: figures/water_energy_frontier_data.csv and results/water_energy_frontier.csv.
