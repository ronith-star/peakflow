# Figure A1 (falls_plymouth_detail_v2), appendix and presentation only

**Figure A1.** The maps show two planned data center sites and the municipal treatment plants nearest to them. Panel A covers Lower Bucks County, Pennsylvania. Site 16 (AWS Keystone Trade Center, Falls Township) is matched to the Trenton Sewer Utility in New Jersey, 1.93 miles away. That plant's median flow of 8.84 million gallons per day covers 665 MW of IT load. The nearest eligible Pennsylvania plant, the Morrisville Borough STP, is 3 miles away. Its flow of 4.21 million gallons per day covers 317 MW. Panel B covers Conshohocken, Plymouth Township, Pennsylvania. Site 17 is matched to the Matsunk STP, 0.78 miles away, whose flow of 2.57 million gallons per day covers 193 MW [epa_echo, trackdatacenters_2026]. Symbols, model and projection are as in figure 1.

The citation keys are epa_echo, trackdatacenters_2026, usgs_wbd, usgs_nhdplus_hr and census_tiger.
