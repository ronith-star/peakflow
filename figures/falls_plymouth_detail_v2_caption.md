# Figure A1 (falls_plymouth_detail_v2), appendix and presentation only

**Figure A1.** Maps showing two planned data center sites and the municipal treatment plants nearest to them. (A) Lower Bucks County, Pennsylvania: site 16 (AWS Keystone Trade Center, Falls Township) is matched to the Trenton Sewer Utility in New Jersey, 1.93 miles away, with a median flow of 8.84 million gallons per day that covers 665 MW of IT load; the nearest eligible Pennsylvania plant, the Morrisville Borough STP, is 3 miles away with 4.21 million gallons per day, covering 317 MW. (B) Conshohocken, Plymouth Township, Pennsylvania: site 17 is matched to the Matsunk STP, 0.78 miles away, with 2.57 million gallons per day covering 193 MW [epa_echo; trackdatacenters_2026]. Symbols, model and projection are as in figure 1.
