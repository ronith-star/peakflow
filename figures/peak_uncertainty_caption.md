# Figure X (peak_uncertainty)

**Figure X.** Range chart showing the simulated peak-day cooling makeup of each of the 24 active planned data center sites in the Delaware River Basin [trackdatacenters_2026] against the median effluent flow of the nearest eligible municipal plant within 10 miles [epa_echo]. For each site the dot marks the 50th percentile (P50) of 10,000 Monte Carlo draws, the light bar spans P50 to the 90th percentile (P90), the tick marks P90 [reuse_ready_model], and the open square marks the plant's median monthly flow converted to gallons per day [epa_echo]. Rows are sorted by P50 and labelled with the site number of the map key [site_key.csv]; gray rows are the 2 sites with no eligible plant within 10 miles [supply_screen.csv]. The right-hand notes give each site's status: P90 exceeds the plant's median flow at 21 sites, the plant's median flow covers P90 at 1 site, and 2 sites have no plant within 10 miles [peak_uncertainty.csv]. The horizontal axis is logarithmic.

Each of the 10,000 draws (random seed 20260925) [peak_uncertainty.json] selects a cooling architecture with LBNL's 2023 hyperscale cooling-system shares grouped into evaporative tower (0.016), hybrid (0.914) and air-cooled chiller (0.070) [shehabi_2024, p. 39]; an annual site water usage effectiveness (WUE) from LBNL's large-scale simulation ranges, 1.72 to 2.78 liters per kilowatt-hour (median 2.18) for the evaporative tower and 0.00 to 1.56 liters per kilowatt-hour (median 0.42) for the hybrid [shehabi_2024, p. 46], with zero makeup for the air-cooled chiller [reuse_ready_model]; and a power usage effectiveness uniform on 1.15 to 1.35 [shehabi_2024, p. 48]. Annual WUE is converted to peak-day makeup with the calibrated cooling model's ratio of maximum day to mean day on Trenton weather for 2005 to 2024, 1.0 for the evaporative tower and 32.6 for the calibrated hybrid [reuse_ready_model; noaa_isd], and hybrid draws are capped at full evaporation of the day's heat, a cap that binds in 92 percent of hybrid draws [reuse_ready_model]. Sites that state a capacity use it; the 16 sites that do not draw their IT load with equal probability from the 8 stated values, 242 to 1,250 MW [peak_uncertainty.json; trackdatacenters_2026]. Plant flows are median monthly discharge reported from July 2023 to June 2026 [epa_echo]. The median flow covers P90 at 1 of 24 sites, against 7 of 24 matchable sites in the deterministic screen [peak_uncertainty.csv; supply_screen.csv]; the sites that change are Comcast Moorestown (DRB04), Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst (DRB05), AWS Keystone Trade Center Campus (DRB12), Sellersville / West Rockhill Township Data Center (DRB14), NorthPoint Spring Mountain Data Center (DRB16), Conshohocken Data Center (DRB31) [peak_uncertainty.csv]. These values are model-derived.

**Citation keys.** trackdatacenters_2026, epa_echo, reuse_ready_model, shehabi_2024, noaa_isd. Plotted values are in figures/peak_uncertainty_data.csv.
