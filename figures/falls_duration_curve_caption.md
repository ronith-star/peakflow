# Figure 3 (falls_duration_curve)

**Figure 3.** Duration curve of modelled daily cooling makeup for the calibrated hybrid system at the Falls Township (AWS Keystone) campus, KTTN weather 2005 to 2024 [reuse_ready_model; noaa_isd; stull_2011]. The model uses the primary peak-day calibration (switchover wet-bulb 22.38 °C, part-load exponent 0.429, 331.2 MW of IT load, PUE 1.2, 4 cycles of concentration) [reuse_ready_model]. Each local calendar day is plotted at its Weibull plotting position, 100 i / (n + 1), among the 7,204 valid days; 101 days with fewer than 20 valid weather hours are excluded [reuse_ready_model]. Makeup is zero on 6,238 days (86.6 percent of valid days), when the hybrid cools without water; these days cannot be drawn on the logarithmic axis, so the curve stops at 13.4 percent of days exceeded and the zero-makeup range is shaded [reuse_ready_model]. Reference lines mark the reported average of 135,000 gal/d [falls_levittown_2026], the reported peak of 4.4 million gal/d [falls_levittown_2026; falls_herald_2026] and the 100,000 gal/d DRBC review threshold, which applies to the daily average over any 30 consecutive days [drbc_admin_manual]. The modelled maximum day (4,400,000 gal/d on 2011-07-22) is 32.6 times the modelled mean (135,000 gal/d) [reuse_ready_model]. Modelled makeup exceeds 100,000 gal on 12.0 percent of valid days (866 days) and 135,000 gal on 11.7 percent (840 days); no day exceeds 4.4 million gal, which the calibration reproduces on 1 day [reuse_ready_model].
