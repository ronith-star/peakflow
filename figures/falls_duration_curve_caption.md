# Figure 3 (falls_duration_curve)

**Figure 3.**

The duration curve shows modelled daily cooling makeup for the calibrated hybrid system at the Falls Township (AWS Keystone) campus under KTTN weather for 2005 to 2024 [reuse_ready_model, noaa_isd, stull_2011]. The model uses the primary peak-day calibration (switchover wet-bulb 22.38 °C, part-load exponent 0.429, 331.2 MW of IT load, PUE 1.2, 4 cycles of concentration) [reuse_ready_model]. Each local calendar day is plotted at its Weibull plotting position, 100 i / (n + 1), among the 7,204 valid days, and the 101 days with fewer than 20 valid weather hours are excluded [reuse_ready_model]. On some days the hybrid cools without water, and these zero-makeup days cannot be drawn on the logarithmic axis. Makeup is zero on 6,238 days (86.6 percent of valid days), so the curve stops at 13.4 percent of days exceeded and the zero-makeup range is shaded [reuse_ready_model]. Reference lines mark the reported average of 135,000 gal/d [falls_levittown_2026], the reported peak of 4.4 million gal/d [falls_levittown_2026, falls_herald_2026] and the 100,000 gal/d DRBC review threshold, which applies to the daily average over any 30 consecutive days [drbc_admin_manual]. The modelled maximum day (4,400,000 gal/d on 2011-07-22) is 32.6 times the modelled mean (135,000 gal/d) [reuse_ready_model]. Modelled makeup exceeds 100,000 gal on 12.0 percent of valid days (866 days) and 135,000 gal on 11.7 percent (840 days) [reuse_ready_model]. No day exceeds 4.4 million gal, which the calibration reproduces on 1 day [falls_figures_summary.json].

The citation keys are reuse_ready_model, noaa_isd, stull_2011, falls_levittown_2026, falls_herald_2026 and drbc_admin_manual.
