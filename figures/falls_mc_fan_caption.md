# Figure X (falls_mc_fan)

**Figure X.**

The line chart shows modeled daily cooling makeup at the Falls Township (AWS Keystone) campus by day of year for the calibrated hybrid cooling architecture [peakflow_model, falls_levittown_2026]. Each thin trace is one parameter draw run over one weather year chosen at random from 2005 to 2024 [falls_mc_fan.json]. Each run uses hourly Trenton (KTTN) wet-bulb temperature on local calendar days [noaa_isd, stull_2011]. The chart draws 300 of 2,000 draws (seed 20260926), and the drawn traces were selected with seed 20260927 [falls_mc_fan.json]. The shaded band spans the 10th to the 90th percentile, and the solid line is the 50th percentile across all 2,000 draws for each day of year [peakflow_model].

Parameters are drawn independently. IT load is uniform on 253 to 331.2 megawatts, from the unverified 253 megawatt figure [cleanview_keystone_2026] to the peak-day calibration's fitted load [peakflow_model]. Power usage effectiveness is uniform on 1.15 to 1.35 [shehabi_2024, p. 48]. The part-load exponent is uniform on 0.355 to 0.429, the design-rate and peak-day calibration readings [peakflow_model]. The switchover wet-bulb temperature is fixed at 22.38 degrees Celsius, the value shared by both readings [peakflow_model]. Cycles of concentration are fixed at 4 [falls_mc_fan.json].

Days with zero makeup cannot be shown on the logarithmic axis and are plotted at a floor of 1,000 gallons per day [falls_mc_fan.json]. They are 86.6 percent of all trace-days, so the 50th percentile sits at the floor on all but 39 days of the year [falls_mc_fan.json]. Dashed reference lines mark the DRBC review trigger of 100,000 gallons per day [drbc_admin_manual] and the reported peak of 4.4 million gallons per day [falls_levittown_2026, falls_herald_2026]. The gray panel marks June to August. Across draws, the largest day of the drawn year has a median of 2,880,953 gallons per day (10th to 90th percentile 2,035,472 to 3,807,230) [falls_mc_fan.json]. The largest day exceeds the trigger in 100 percent of draws [falls_mc_fan.json]. Day 366 exists only in leap years [noaa_isd]. These values are model-derived.

The citation keys are peakflow_model, falls_levittown_2026, falls_herald_2026, noaa_isd, stull_2011, cleanview_keystone_2026, shehabi_2024 and drbc_admin_manual. Plotted values are in figures/falls_mc_fan_data.csv.
